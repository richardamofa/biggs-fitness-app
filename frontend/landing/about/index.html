Our story

Built by People
Who Love Fitness

Biggs Fitness was born from a simple belief — that everyone deserves access to quality fitness guidance, regardless of their gym membership or budget. We started as a small team of fitness enthusiasts and developers who wanted to make structured, intelligent training accessible to anyone with a phone.

Today, we combine simple AI-powered workout planning with human coaching to help you build sustainable habits, track real progress, and stay motivated even on the days you don't feel like it.

What we stand for

Our Mission

We believe fitness should be accessible, adaptable, and results-driven. Our mission is to remove every barrier between you and your best self — whether that's cost, equipment, time, or know-how.

Results First

Every program is built around measurable outcomes, not just motivation. We track, adjust, and keep you moving forward.

Community Driven

Fitness is better together. Our platform connects members, coaches, and supporters who keep each other accountable.

Fully Flexible

No gym? No time? No problem. Our plans adapt to your schedule, equipment, and goals — not the other way around.

AI-Powered

Smart training plans generated by AI, refined by coaches, and tailored to how your body actually responds.
