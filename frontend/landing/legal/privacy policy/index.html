Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

At Biggs Fitness, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it simple — no legal jargon, no surprises.

1. What We Collect

When you create an account and use Biggs Fitness, we collect:

  • Account info — your name and email address when you sign up
  • Profile data — fitness level, goal, equipment preference and workout days you set during onboarding
  • Workout data — sessions you complete, exercises, duration and calories burned
  • Progress data — streaks, total sessions, total minutes and calories over time
  • Device info — browser type and approximate location (country level) for analytics

We do not collect payment card details. Payments are processed securely by Paystack.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to:

  • Generate personalised workout plans and quick sessions through our AI
  • Track your progress, streaks and fitness history
  • Improve the app based on usage patterns
  • Send optional workout reminders (only if you allow notifications)
  • Verify payments and manage your subscription plan

We never sell your personal data to third parties. Ever.

3. AI and Your Data

When you generate a workout plan or quick session, your fitness profile (level, goal, equipment) is sent to our AI provider (Groq) to generate a response. This data is used only to generate your plan and is not stored by the AI provider beyond the request.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely in Supabase, a PostgreSQL database with row-level security. This means only you can read and write your own data. We use HTTPS for all data in transit.

Your password is never stored in plain text — Supabase Auth handles authentication using industry-standard hashing.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — database and authentication
  • Groq — AI workout plan generation
  • Paystack — payment processing for Pro and Elite plans
  • Google — optional Google sign-in (OAuth)

Each of these services has their own privacy policy that governs how they handle data.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to cache your name and streak count for a faster experience. This data stays on your device and is cleared when you log out. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export your workout history
  • Opt out of notifications at any time through your browser settings

To request account deletion or a data export, email us at biggsdevs@gmail.com and we will process your request within 7 business days.

8. Children

Biggs Fitness is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will remove it immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

Questions about your privacy? Reach us at biggsdevs@gmail.com or through the contact form on our homepage.
